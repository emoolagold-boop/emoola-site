The Network Model
EMOOLA operates as a network: each data sector is structured as its own independent company — a “node” — led by a sector-expert director who holds 51% ownership of that node. EMOOLA provides the platform, brand licence, compliance framework, and central support. The director is the principal of their own sector business within the network, not an employee.
Who We Are Looking For
We are seeking senior professionals from the financial-data-services world who bring genuine sector expertise and genuine client relationships — people whose existing customers would realistically follow them to a better-priced specialist data offering. This is a principal-level ownership opportunity in an early-stage venture, for someone who believes in the model and brings their own market. It is not a salaried post.
